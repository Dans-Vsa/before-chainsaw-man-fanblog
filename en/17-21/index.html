Overview#

This volume gathers Fujimoto’s work from his rookie-contest days up to the Jump SQ supplements. The art is still rough, but his storytelling instinct is already there: absurd premises played completely straight, then suddenly turning heartfelt.

The mix is wide: the last humans disguised as chickens, a student who stops a bullet for his teacher, a love confession in the student council room, and an assassin who fails to kill an immortal vampire.

01A Couple Clucking Chickens Were Still Kickin' in the Schoolyard#

庭には二羽ニワトリがいた · Niwa ni wa Niwa Niwatori ga Ita

  • Year2011
  • PublishedJump Rookie! (2011)
  • GenreSci-fi, dark comedy

Aliens have conquered Earth and humanity is nearly extinct. In a school now full of alien students, two survivors stay alive by wearing chicken heads and posing as the school’s pet chickens.

A diligent alien student eventually discovers their secret. Instead of reporting them, he decides to help them survive.

Why it stands out: A silly premise that slowly turns into a story about empathy. This "laugh first, hurt later" pattern keeps returning in Fujimoto’s later work. It is also the oldest piece in the collection.

AmiOne of the last humans, hiding behind a chicken head and a cape.YūtoThe second survivor; his chicken head almost never comes off.

02Sasaki Stopped a Bullet#

佐々木くんが銃弾止めた · Sasaki-kun ga Jūdan Tometa

  • Year2013
  • PublishedCrown contest results, Jump SQ (2013)
  • GenreComedy, action

Sasaki idolizes his teacher, Kawaguchi, who taught him to chase his dreams.

When her past threatens her reputation, Sasaki pushes himself to do something seemingly impossible to protect her.

Why it stands out: Over-the-top action logic delivered with a straight face. Beneath the silliness is a sincere story about a student’s faith in his teacher.

SasakiA bespectacled student who believes in his teacher more than anything.KawaguchiThe teacher who taught Sasaki to dream.

03Love Is Blind#

恋は盲目 · Koi wa Mōmoku

  • Year2013
  • PublishedCrown contest results, Jump SQ (2013)
  • GenreRomantic comedy

Ibuki, the student council president, credits all his success to Kōnosu, the council’s only other member.

Before leaving for university he is determined to confess, even if it means putting that personal mission ahead of his own duties.

Why it stands out: A fast, loud rom-com bursting with teenage energy. It is Fujimoto at his sweetest, with comedic timing that feels very "Jump manga".

IbukiThe council president who wants to confess before graduating.KōnosuThe only other council member, and the reason for Ibuki’s success.

04Shikaku#

シカク · Shikaku

  • Year2014
  • PublishedJump SQ supplement, July 2014
  • GenreAction, supernatural drama

Shikaku, an assassin, is hired to kill Yugel, an immortal vampire who has grown tired of living.

She fails, then apologizes for his suffering. Yugel forgives her, and the empathy between them may rekindle his will to live.

Why it stands out: Violence and tenderness in one story, led by an innocent, peculiar heroine. Many readers see early versions of Chainsaw Man’s characters here.

ShikakuA pink-haired assassin, innocent and hard to read.YugelAn immortal vampire who has lived far too long.

Anime adaptation#

All four stories in this volume are part of the anthology anime 藤本タツキ 17-26 (2025), which had a limited theatrical run in Japan from October 17, 2025 and premiered worldwide on Prime Video on November 8, 2025.
